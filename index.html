Welcome to our science exhibition prototype! SmartCart is an intelligent AI-powered shopping companion designed to transform supermarket shopping into an effortless, budget-friendly, and streamlined experience.

Explore our interactive demo, scientific principles, empirical research, and project process using the top navigation bar.

Problem Statement & Objective

The Problem: Navigating large modern supermarkets causes friction for shoppers. Finding specific products, tracking spending against a budget in real-time, comparing unit prices, and managing dietary requirements are frequently tedious and time-consuming.

Our Objective: SmartCart combines automated budget tracking, real-time item dynamic calculation, smart alternative recommendations, and rack location guidance into an intuitive assistant interface.

Scientific Principles Used

Artificial Intelligence

Drives smart recommendations, evaluating cart content against customer dietary preferences and constraints.

Natural Language Processing

Translates verbal/text commands like “Find snacks under ₹300” into structured inventory database queries.

Data Processing

Indexes multi-variable datasets including item codes, price points, metric quantities, and aisle coordinates.

Mathematics & Automation

Executes continuous balance calculations, dynamic threshold monitoring, and optimal route sequencing.

Execution Steps & Workflow

  1. Creates or imports a personalized interactive shopping list.
  2. Scans and checks real-time prices against the store database.
  3. Calculates total running cost dynamically as items enter the cart.
  4. Monitors total cost against user-configured budget limits.
  5. Suggests alternative products if budget thresholds are exceeded.
  6. Provides exact aisle and rack location vectors for item retrieval.
  7. Generates an itemized final summary with automated digital checkout setup.
