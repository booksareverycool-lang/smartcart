Welcome to SmartCart

Welcome to our science exhibition prototype! SmartCart is an intelligent AI-powered shopping companion designed to transform supermarket shopping into an effortless, budget-friendly, and optimized experience.

Explore our interactive simulator, dynamic store map route planner, and project insights using the top navigation bar.

Problem Statement & Objective

The Problem: Navigating large modern supermarkets causes friction for shoppers. Finding specific items, tracking real-time cart spending against budgets, and identifying suitable dietary or budget alternatives can be exhausting.

Our Objective: SmartCart combines automated budget calculation, real-time item dynamic tracking, smart alternative recommendations, and indoor aisle navigation into a seamless interface.

Research & Market Insights

Statistic Insight / Survey Source
70% Of shoppers surveyed said finding the right product in large store aisles is the most time-consuming step.
66% Of Indian consumers in a PwC survey use smartphones to research prices, offers, and availability before buying.
62% Visit physical stores for product discovery, while 65% use search engines to research products.
40% Of surveyed Indian shoppers switch stores for better deals, according to NielsenIQ grocery research.

Scientific Principles Used

Artificial Intelligence

Drives smart recommendations, evaluating cart content against user-configured budget limits.

Graph Theory & Route Optimization

Utilizes shortest-path algorithms to calculate the most time-efficient trajectory across store nodes.

Data Processing

Indexes multi-variable datasets including item codes, price points, unit quantities, and rack coordinates.

Automated Logic

Executes continuous balance calculations, dynamic threshold monitoring, and automated alternative suggestions.
