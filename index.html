SmartCart AI

AI Shopping Assistant & Supermarket Navigation System

Welcome to SmartCart AI Assistant

Welcome to our science exhibition prototype! SmartCart AI is an intelligent shopping companion designed to transform supermarket shopping into an effortless, personalized, budget-friendly, and optimized experience.

Our project addresses modern shopping challenges by providing personalized product recommendations, real-time budget management, detailed product comparisons, and indoor supermarket route navigation.

Problem Statement & Project Aim

The Problem: Shopping in large modern supermarkets can be time-consuming and confusing. Customers frequently struggle to locate specific products, stay strictly within their preset budget, compare price-to-quality alternatives, and identify items matching their specific dietary or personal preferences.

Our Project Aim: Our project aims to develop an AI-powered Shopping Assistant that understands customer instructions and provides:

  • Personalised Product Recommendations: Tailored to customer requirements and queries.
  • Automated Budget Management: Real-time spent-versus-limit tracking with warning alerts.
  • Smart Alternative Swapping: Instant suggestions when items are out of stock or exceed budget limits.
  • Indoor Store Navigation: Dynamic shortest-path mapping through aisle racks.

Assistant Workflow Steps / Process

  1. Create Shopping List: Establishes a structured shopping list based on user selection or queries.
  2. Price Inspection: Automatically checks and verifies unit prices across selected inventory items.
  3. Cost Calculation: Dynamically calculates subtotal and running spending sum.
  4. Budget Comparison: Continuously evaluates total expenditure against the customer's preset budget limit.
  5. Smart Suggestions: Proactively suggests budget-friendly alternatives if the budget limit is breached or items are unavailable.
  6. Supermarket Navigation: Maps exact rack locations and plots shortest aisle paths.
  7. Final Summary Generation: Produces a complete shopping itemized receipt/summary upon checkout.

Scientific Principles Used

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Helps the system make smart recommendations based on specific customer requirements and constraints.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Allows the assistant to understand human natural instructions, such as “Find snacks under ₹300.”

Data Processing

Organises multi-variable product information such as price points, categories, availability, and rack coordinates.

Mathematics & Graph Logic

Calculates total costs, budget headroom, and shortest path algorithms for efficient aisle navigation.

Automation

Executes automated tasks like list creation, real-time threshold monitoring, and alternative product swapping.
