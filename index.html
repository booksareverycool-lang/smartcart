About Us

The Problem

Traditional supermarket navigation leads to inefficient paths, crowded aisles, unnecessary walking, and wasted customer time searching for individual items across complex floor plans.

Our Solution

An intelligent, IoT-enabled navigation framework that pairs smart cart hardware with optimized pathfinding algorithms to compute the shortest path through all selected items in real time.

How It Works

As items are selected from the inventory, the system calculates their physical rack locations and generates an animated, dynamic waypoint route directly from your start point to checkout.

Key Applications

Ideal for modern retail hypermarkets, automated micro-fulfillment centers, industrial warehouse pick-and-pack operations, and accessibility assistance in large commercial venues.
