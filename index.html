⚡ Queue-Busting Automated Checkout

Shifts checkout overhead directly to the cart. Generates real-time digital receipts with tax breakdown and mobile payment integration to eliminate register lines.

🗺️ Shortest-Path Route Optimization

Uses coordinate matrix spatial indexing (Aisle/Rack/Shelf) and pathfinding logic to route shoppers efficiently through supermarket layouts.

🏷️ Unit Price Value Engine

Compares name-brand products against generic alternatives on a normalized per-unit metric basis (price per 100g/mL), allowing instant smart cost swaps.

Built upon a structured, metric-enabled catalog featuring generic and brand-name alternatives, spatial coordinates, and price matrices.
