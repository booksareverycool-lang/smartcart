Problem & Objective

Shopping shouldn't
be exhausting.

Large supermarkets are confusing. People struggle to find products, stay on budget, compare prices, and spot items that fit their diet. SmartCart is an AI assistant that understands plain instructions and helps shoppers finish faster, spend less, and walk less.

Personalised recommendations

Tailored to each shopper's requirements and queries.

Automated budget management

Real-time spent-versus-limit tracking with warnings.

Smart alternative swapping

Instant suggestions when items are out of stock or too expensive.

Indoor store navigation

Shortest-path mapping through every aisle rack.

Assistant Workflow

From list to receipt.

Create shopping list

Builds a structured list from selections or queries.

Price inspection

Verifies unit prices across selected items.

Cost calculation

Tracks subtotal and running spend.

Budget comparison

Checks total spend against your limit continuously.

Smart suggestions

Proposes cheaper alternatives when limits or stock are a problem.

Supermarket navigation

Maps rack locations and plots the shortest aisle path.

Final summary

Produces an itemised receipt at checkout.
