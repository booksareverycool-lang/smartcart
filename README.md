AI SHOPPING ASSISTANT

Problem
Shopping in large supermarkets can be time-consuming and confusing. Customers may struggle to find products, stay within their budget, compare alternatives, and identify products according to their preferences or requirements.
Our project aims to develop an AI-powered Shopping Assistant that understands a customer's requirements and provides personalised product recommendations, budget management, product information, and store navigation assistance.
Data
70% of online shoppers surveyed in India said finding the right product/service is the most time-consuming part of online shopping. 
66% of Indian consumers in a PwC survey said they frequently or almost always use their smartphones to research products for prices, offers and availability before buying. 
In PwC's 2024 India survey, 62% of consumers said they visit physical stores for product discovery, while 65% use search engines to research products.
40% of surveyed Indian shoppers switch stores for better deals, according to NielsenIQ's 2024 India grocery research. 
KPMG's 2025 India grocery customer-experience research found that 22% of customers feel that having everything they need in one place saves time and effort. 
Scientific principles
The project uses principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), automation, data processing, and basic mathematical calculations.
Artificial Intelligence: Helps the system make recommendations based on the customer's requirements.
Natural Language Processing: Allows the assistant to understand human instructions such as “Find snacks under ₹300.”
Data Processing: Organises product information such as price, category, availability, and location.
Mathematics: Calculates the total cost of products and helps the customer stay within a specified budget.
Automation: Performs tasks such as creating a shopping list and suggesting suitable alternatives.
Steps/Process
Creates a suitable shopping list.
Check the prices of the selected products.
Calculates the total cost.
Compare the total with the customer's budget.
Suggests alternatives if the budget is exceeded or a product is unavailable.
Provides the location of the products in the supermarket.
Generates a final shopping summary.

Future Scope
In the future, our AI Shopping Assistant can become even smarter and more useful. It could help customers find products using voice commands, check whether products are available, suggest cheaper or better alternatives, and guide customers around the store. It could also be connected to smart shopping carts and payment systems, making shopping faster, easier, and more convenient.
